# Calibration

## The limitation, stated plainly

Existential and civilizational outcomes have not occurred in a way that permits ordinary calibration. No forecaster, survey or model has a track record on "human extinction by 2100". Nothing on this site claims such calibration, and proxy calibration (below) does not transfer to it. This is why the official value is withheld and why the calibration gap term dominates the uncertainty score.

## Proxy calibration plan

Resolvable proxy questions will be registered and scored:

- capability milestones (benchmark thresholds, time-horizon crossings);
- release and policy milestones (framework updates, laws taking effect, enforcement actions);
- incident occurrence in registries;
- deployment adoption and compute trends.

Scores tracked per forecaster and per model component: Brier score, log score, calibration curve, sharpness, resolution, missing predictions, revisions, expired forecasts and forecaster attrition. Records will live in a `calibration.json` file of a future snapshot; the `calibration_gap` component then becomes $1 -$ (share of research-mode estimates whose parameters are tied to scored proxies).

## What would allow an official probability

A documented mapping from proxy performance to long-horizon estimates that survives adversarial review, plus stable compatibility groups with more than a handful of members. Until then the honest headline is "insufficiently calibrated".
