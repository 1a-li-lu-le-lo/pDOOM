# Content-safety policy

High-level risk education is permitted. The following is never published in code, data, docs or UI: biological protocols, pathogen design, laboratory optimisation, chemical synthesis for harm, exploit payloads, zero-day procedures, critical-infrastructure targeting, autonomous-weapons implementation, environmental sabotage mechanisms, evasion methods, model-weight theft procedures, operational coercion strategies.

When a dangerous pathway is covered (S4, S5, S6, S7, S11), the record explains only: the category; why experts monitor it; observable, non-operational indicators; safeguards; policy implications; response resources. Incident summaries are non-graphic and omit methods. Security records redact live secrets, unpatched exploit procedures, targets and weaponisable details, and respect coordinated disclosure.

Review checklist for every record: (1) could a motivated reader act on this text to cause harm? (2) is every detail necessary for a reader to understand risk and safeguards? (3) does it name a technique, target, quantity, parameter or sequence? If any answer is yes, remove the detail and note the removal in `content_safety_note`.

Psychological safety: calm language; no inevitability; beneficial scenarios and interventions shown prominently; no countdowns, alerts or graphic imagery (`docs/governance/psychological-safety.md`).
