# Cassandra review: release rel-2026-09-26-001

## Findings

### CR-001 · critical · accepted_risk · Reviewer independence not demonstrated

- **Component:** `data/releases/rel-2026-09-26-001/approvals.json`, `data/keys/reviewers/`
- **Claim under attack:** "Two distinct reviewers approved this release under heightened review."
- **Assumption:** The two ed25519 keys (`nuc-editorial-dev`, `nuc-methodology-dev`) belong to two independent people.
- **Counterevidence:** Both key pairs were generated in the same build session by the same actor; the `-dev` suffix marks them as development keys. The gate in `internal/publishing` checks distinctness of key identities, not of people.
- **Reproduction:** `go run ./cmd/pdoomctl release list` and `go run ./cmd/pdoomctl audit verify data/audit/audit.jsonl` pass; read `data/releases/rel-2026-09-26-001/approvals.json` and note that both key ids end in `-dev` and were created in the same session.
- **Impact:** The governance guarantee "no automatic publication, two independent signatures" holds mechanically but not socially for this release.
- **Recommendation:** Before any non-prototype release, issue reviewer keys to named people through the procedure in `docs/operations/runbook.md`, retire the `-dev` keys, and add the reviewer roster with key fingerprints to `docs/governance/editorial-policy.md`.
- **Status:** accepted_risk for the research prototype; recorded on the release page (`/releases/rel-2026-09-26-001`, approvals section shows key ids).

### CR-002 · high · open · Median-of-two is not "robust to a single extreme forecast"

- **Component:** `internal/model/aggregate.go` (aggregation note text), `data/releases/rel-2026-09-26-001/aggregations.json`
- **Claim under attack:** The preferred-method note says the unweighted median is "robust to a single extreme forecast".
- **Assumption:** Groups contain enough members for a median to discard extremes.
- **Counterevidence:** All five compatibility groups have `n = 2`. The median of two values is their midpoint, and the leave-one-source-out runs show it: removing either member of `G-EXT-2100-METACULUS` moves the value from 0.1475 to 0.075 or to 0.22; for `G-CAT10-2100` from 0.0707 to 0.0213 or to 0.12.
- **Reproduction:** `jq '.items[] | select(.preferred) | {group_id, n}' data/releases/rel-2026-09-26-001/aggregations.json`; compare with `sensitivity.json` runs of kind `leave_one_source_out`.
- **Impact:** A reader of the note could believe the aggregate is stable when it is fully determined by whichever two forecasts were admitted.
- **Recommendation:** Make the note conditional on `n`: for `n < 3` say "with two members the median is their midpoint; the leave-one-out runs show its full dependence on each member". The web and text pages already print `n` and the population count next to every aggregate, and the meter's "why" section ranks the leave-one-out runs first.
- **Status:** open; requires a model text change and therefore a new release.

### CR-003 · high · accepted_risk · Two groups cover the same outcome and horizon and differ by a factor of about eight

- **Component:** `G-EXT-2100` (0.0169) and `G-EXT-2100-METACULUS` (0.1475), both outcome set `O6`, horizon `2100`.
- **Claim under attack:** "Forecasts are only combined when they ask about the same question."
- **Assumption:** Same outcome set and horizon implies the same question.
- **Counterevidence:** The two groups are split by population and platform (tournament experts and superforecasters versus Metaculus forecaster pools), which is a deliberate rule in `research/forecasts/compatibility-groups.md`, but a reader sees two "external aggregates" for extinction by 2100 that disagree by roughly eight times.
- **Reproduction:** `/forecasts#G-EXT-2100` and `/forecasts#G-EXT-2100-METACULUS`.
- **Impact:** Cherry-picking either group would support opposite narratives.
- **Recommendation:** Keep both; never merge them. The presentation shows both with their group ids and population counts, and the disagreement label on each is `high` or `extreme`. Add a one-line explanation of the population split to the group heading in the next release's `conditioning` text.
- **Status:** accepted_risk (documented in `research/forecasts/compatibility-groups.md`).

### CR-004 · high · accepted_risk · Provenance without archives or content hashes

- **Component:** `data/snapshots/snap-2026-09-26-001/sources.json`
- **Claim under attack:** "Every source is verified."
- **Assumption:** Verification means the page was fetched and hashed.
- **Counterevidence:** 53 of 70 sources are `verified_search` (a search-result snippet confirmed title, publisher and the quoted figure; the page itself could not be fetched from the build environment), 17 are `verified_prior_knowledge` and none are `verified_fetch`. `archive_reference` is null for 70 of 70 and `content_hash` for 70 of 70; `robots_status` is `unknown` for 58.
- **Reproduction:** `jq '[.items[].verification.status] | group_by(.) | map({(.[0]): length})' data/snapshots/snap-2026-09-26-001/sources.json`.
- **Impact:** A source could have changed or been retracted since the snippet was indexed, and no byte-level evidence of what was read exists.
- **Recommendation:** Run `pdoom-ingest --allow-network --check-robots` from an environment with egress, archive each canonical URL, fill `content_hash` and `archive_reference`, and promote a new snapshot. Until then the model uses only items whose status is `verified_search` (the 17 prior-knowledge items are `informational` and excluded from every computation, which the validator enforces).
- **Status:** accepted_risk; the source ledger (`/evidence`) displays the status of every item.

### CR-005 · high · resolved · Web pages were prerendered at build time

- **Component:** `apps/web/app/layout.tsx`
- **Claim under attack:** "A promotion or rollback is served within seconds."
- **Assumption:** The web app reads `data/releases/CURRENT` per request as the API does.
- **Counterevidence:** The first production build marked every route static (`○`), so a rollback would have kept showing the rolled-back numbers until a rebuild.
- **Reproduction:** `cd apps/web && npx next build` and read the route table.
- **Impact:** The rollback guarantee in `docs/governance/update-governance.md` would not have held for the web surface.
- **Recommendation:** Render per request (`export const dynamic = "force-dynamic"` in the root layout); the SDK re-reads `CURRENT` at most every five seconds.
- **Status:** resolved in this branch.

### CR-006 · medium · accepted_risk · Nine of seventeen driver observations are judgments

- **Component:** `driver_observations.json`; indexes `control_strength`, `capability_pressure`, `agentic_infrastructure_risk`
- **Claim under attack:** "Indexes measure movement in the evidence."
- **Counterevidence:** 9 of 17 observations have `observation_kind: judgment`; mean confidence across observations is 0.53; `control_strength` has coverage 0.85 because specified signals lacked eligible observations.
- **Impact:** Index values are partly editorial. The uncertainty score already carries this through the `judgment_share` component, which is why the release's uncertainty score is 56 and the label is `high`.
- **Recommendation:** Replace judgments with observations as sources become fetchable; publish the observation/judgment split next to every gauge (the signal tables on `/capabilities`, `/agents` and `/safeguards` do).
- **Status:** accepted_risk.

### CR-007 · medium · open · The research-mode outcome shares are a single judgment

- **Component:** `model_spec.experimental_causal.horizons.*.O`
- **Claim under attack:** "The decomposition into O3–O8 is model output."
- **Counterevidence:** Conditional outcome shares (O3 p50 0.25, O4 0.10, O5 0.04, O6 0.05, O7 0.02, O8 0.10 at 10y) are one documented judgment; the rationale cites the gradual-disempowerment literature and survey medians but no elicitation procedure. The intervals shown therefore express the width of that judgment, not disagreement between people.
- **Impact:** The decomposition on the meter could be read as evidence about relative likelihoods when it is a prior.
- **Recommendation:** Run a small structured elicitation (at least three independent respondents per parameter) before the next model version and record it as sources; until then, keep the "Parameters are documented judgments" note on every research-mode card (present).
- **Status:** open (requires a new model version, which is a heightened-review trigger).

### CR-008 · medium · resolved · A degenerate interval rendered as a range

- **Component:** `internal/model/run.go` (`intervalOrPoint`), `apps/web/lib/format.ts`
- **Counterevidence:** The 10-year biospheric-catastrophe research estimate displays its interval as `<1%–<1%`.
- **Impact:** Reads as a range that is not one; a small credibility cost.
- **Recommendation:** Collapse equal endpoints in the model formatter for the next release. The web surfaces now normalise the display string (`tidyInterval`) without touching the data.
- **Status:** resolved in the presentation; the release file is unchanged by design.

### CR-009 · medium · accepted_risk · Forecast staleness

- **Component:** `forecasts.json`
- **Counterevidence:** Member forecasts date from 2020-03-05 to 2026-09-23; the `eventual` groups rest on 2022 survey items and one 2021 item.
- **Impact:** Old forecasts carry old capability expectations.
- **Recommendation:** Show `forecast_origin_date` on every card (done) and add a staleness term to the uncertainty score in the next model version.
- **Status:** accepted_risk.

### CR-010 · low · resolved · Scene and comparator copy could imply scale

- **Component:** `apps/web/app/compare/page.tsx`, hero copy
- **Counterevidence:** Early drafts offered everyday-risk comparisons; these have measured base rates and existential outcomes do not.
- **Recommendation:** Offer only re-expressions of the same estimate ("N of one thousand futures", "one in N", the complement) and say why no analogy is offered.
- **Status:** resolved; the guard test in `apps/web/test/no-hardcoded-numbers.test.ts` prevents fixed percentages from re-entering the source.

### CR-011 · low · open · The `attention` index is declared but not computed

- **Component:** `indexes.json` (`attention`, value null, coverage 0)
- **Impact:** None for readers (the meter omits it), but the schema promises seven indexes and the release delivers six.
- **Recommendation:** Either specify the attention signals in the next model spec or remove the index from the enum.
- **Status:** open.

## Checks that passed

- Invariants: p(DOOM) is at least the largest single outcome at every horizon; all quantiles are ordered; every research-mode estimate is monotone non-decreasing across horizons; the official object has null quantiles at all seven horizons.
- Separation: no estimate blends statuses; indexes carry `is_probability: false`; the Scenario Lab labels every result `user_scenario` and the MCP and web tests assert it.
- Content: scenario records contain no procedures; incident summaries are non-graphic; no countdown, deadline or inevitability language appears in the web source (guard test).
- Reproducibility: promotion re-ran the model and compared byte for byte; `reproduction_command` is in the manifest.
- Tier discipline: no `used` source carries `developer_self_report`; tier 4–5 items are `informational` or `excluded`.
